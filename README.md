### 📌 Overview & SEO Summary
Welcome to the ultimate curated directory of **generative AI music keyboards**, **open-source music generation models**, and **AI-assisted composition frameworks**. Whether you are looking for commercial AI music platforms (such as *Suno*, *Udio*, and *AIVA*), or self-hostable open-source alternatives (like *ACE-Step*, *Magenta RealTime 2*, and *NanoMaestro*), this list covers category leaders, real-time music models, and privacy-respecting creative AI tools.

**Key Market Context:**
- **AWS DeepComposer** was the **first ML-powered music keyboard** for developers, but **support ended September 17, 2025** — existing hardware still works with any DAW as a standard MIDI keyboard.
- **Magenta RealTime 2** is the **leading open-source real-time music model**, with **~0.2s control delay** and **frame-by-frame note/drum conditioning** at **25 Hz**.
- **ACE-Step 1.5** is the **most powerful open-source local music generation model**, supporting **Mac, AMD, Intel, and CUDA devices** with **full song generation from text descriptions**.

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[ACE-Step 1.5](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/stargazers)  
  **The most powerful local music generation model**, MIT licensed. **Outperforms almost all commercial alternatives** — supporting **Mac, AMD, Intel, and CUDA devices**. **Full song generation from simple descriptions** with **query rewriting, audio understanding, and LRC generation**. **Repaint & Edit** for selective local audio editing. **Track separation** into individual stems. **Multi-track generation** like Suno Studio's "Add Layer". **Vocal2BGM** auto-generates accompaniment for vocal tracks. **LoRA training** with 8 songs in 1 hour on 3090 (12GB VRAM). **Gradio Web UI** and **REST API server** included. **The definitive open-source music generation platform**. 🎶

- **[Magenta RealTime 2 (MRT2)](https://github.com/magenta/magenta-realtime)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/magenta/magenta-realtime?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/magenta/magenta-realtime/stargazers)  
  **Open-source real-time live music model by Google**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **~0.2s control delay** — **frame-by-frame conditioning at 25 Hz** (40 ms per frame). **Decoder-only architecture** with **sliding window attention** for continuous streaming generation. **Multi-signal control**: **style (audio or text), note on/off, and drums on/off**. **Steer it with MIDI notes on a keyboard** in real time. **NVIDIA port** available for Windows (WSL2) and native Linux. **The most responsive open-source live music model** — designed for **human-in-the-loop performance**. 🎹

- **[llama.cpp (Music Capabilities)](https://github.com/ggerganov/llama.cpp)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ggerganov/llama.cpp?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ggerganov/llama.cpp/stargazers)  
  **LLM inference in C/C++ with audio model support**, MIT licensed. **70K+ GitHub stars** — **runs on CPU, GPU, and Apple Silicon**. **GGUF quantization** for reduced memory footprint. **Foundation for many local AI tools** including music generation pipelines. 🦙

- **[NanoMaestro Realtime](https://huggingface.co/utkucoban/NanoMaestro-Realtime)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/utkucoban/NanoMaestro-Realtime?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/utkucoban/NanoMaestro-Realtime/stargazers)  
  **Tiny local real-time symbolic piano generator**, CC-BY-NC-4.0 licensed. **Runs continuously in real time on consumer CPUs** — **Light model ~14 MB, Pro model ~54 MB** (INT8 quantized). **Symbolic piano-note event generation** rendered by playback system. **Fully client-side browser inference with WASM**. **Trained on 496M–2.52B tokens** of piano MIDI data. **The most accessible open-source real-time music generator**. 🎹

- **[SqncR](https://github.com/bradygaster/SqncR)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bradygaster/SqncR?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/bradygaster/SqncR/stargazers)  
  **AI-native generative music for MIDI devices**, MIT licensed. **MCP server with 30+ tools** — **AI assistant talks to SqncR via endpoints**. **480 TPQ timing accuracy** — **professional DAW precision**. **Three melody generators**: **ScaleWalk, WeightedNote (musical gravity), and Arpeggio**. **19 scales, 10 scale types**, **5 drum patterns with swing**. **Sonic Pi and VCV Rack integration**. **The most developer-friendly AI music engine** for MIDI devices. 🎛️

- **[theDAW](https://github.com/gantasmo/theDAW)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/gantasmo/theDAW?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/gantasmo/theDAW/stargazers)  
  **Full-featured local-first DAW, DJ, and VJ app with AI**, open-source. **Stable Audio 3, Magenta RealTime 2, Suno API, Chimera track fusion, Demucs stems**. **MIDI generate/notate, image > spectrogram > music, drawing > music**. **VST3 & .gan plugins, automix & key-lock, GLSL shaders, volumetric video, Quest 3 XR interface, RAG assistant**. **Interoperable with Ableton, Reaper, and Resolume**. **The most complete open-source AI music workstation**. 🎛️

- **[KOSMOS](https://github.com/plantssystem/KOSMOS)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/plantssystem/KOSMOS?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/plantssystem/KOSMOS/stargazers)  
  **Generative MIDI sequencer for RP2040**, MIT licensed. **Self-contained generative instrument** on **Raspberry Pi Pico + Waveshare Pico-Audio + Pico-LCD-1.3**. **Scale-dependent behavior (HEI/MIYA/INSEN)** with **smooth, jumping, or deep expressive movement**. **Dual-channel PRA32-U synthesis** with **USB MIDI output**. **Color-coded step bar visualization**. **The most accessible hardware-based generative music instrument**. 🎹

- **[Composer Assistant](https://github.com/attrix182/composer-assistant)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/attrix182/composer-assistant?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/attrix182/composer-assistant/stargazers)  
  **Web-based AI music composition tool**, open-source. **Scale generator** (major, minor, pentatonic, blues). **Chord progression generator** (pop, jazz, rock, blues). **AI melody generation** with **Google Gemini 1.5 Flash**. **MIDI export** for DAW integration. **Hardware MIDI controller support** (Arturia Minilab 3). 🌐

- **[Magenta RT Jam](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/magenta-rt-jam-dev)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/multimodalart/magenta-rt-jam-dev?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/multimodalart/magenta-rt-jam-dev/stargazers)  
  **Play keyboard/MIDI to steer Magenta RealTime 2 in real time**, open-source. **Pure PyTorch implementation** — **note conditioning for live music generation**. **The most accessible way to experience MRT2 real-time control**. 🎹

- **[TinyAudio](https://github.com/TinyAudio/TinyAudio)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/TinyAudio/TinyAudio?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/TinyAudio/TinyAudio/stargazers)  
  **Compact and efficient text-to-audio generation for low-resource deployment**, open-source. **87M parameters total** — **0.48 GB peak GPU memory**. **AudioCaps FAD of 2.65** with **one-step variant at RTF 0.715 on four-core CPU**. **The most efficient open-source audio generation model**. 🔬

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **AWS DeepComposer support ended September 17, 2025** — the **hardware still works as a standard MIDI keyboard** with any DAW, but **console and API access are no longer available**.
- **Magenta RealTime 2 provides ~0.2s control delay** — significantly faster than the original Magenta RealTime's 2-second minimum, enabling **responsive live performance**. **ACE-Step 1.5 outperforms most commercial alternatives** for local music generation.
- **Open-source music generation models are not turnkey** — **ACE-Step requires Python 3.11 and CUDA GPU (12GB+ VRAM recommended)**. **Magenta RT2 requires PyTorch and a compatible GPU**. **NanoMaestro runs on CPU but is symbolic-only (no audio)**. **Always validate output quality and licensing terms** before commercial use . 🎶
